# Goblin Slayer: Gear Catalog

Every world has 12 weapons, 8 armors and 20 accessories, 240 pieces in all. Gear grows with whoever wears it, so the stats below are shown for a level 20 wearer. Tiers: **Common** has a tan name and is sold at that world's pawn shop. **Rare** has a magenta name and glow. **Legendary** has a gold name and glow. Rares and legendaries come from chests, hidden spots, side dungeons, quests and random battle drops.
